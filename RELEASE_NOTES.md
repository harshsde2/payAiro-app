# What's New in Version 1.5

## Enhanced KYC Experience

• **Improved KYC Status Management** - We've enhanced our Know Your Customer (KYC) verification system with better status tracking and notifications. You'll now receive timely alerts when your KYC status requires attention.

• **Streamlined KYC Completion** - Simplified the KYC verification process with clearer guidance and automated navigation to help you complete your verification quickly and easily.

• **Better User Experience** - Enhanced app navigation and interface updates provide a smoother experience when managing your KYC status across different screens.

• **Performance Improvements** - Optimized app performance and error handling for a more reliable and stable experience.
